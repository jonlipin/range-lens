## 1.20.0 - 2026-10-17

- Nameplate previews: tick Focus, Combined or Target in the preview's corner to see your focus's nameplate, the one of a mob that is your target and your focus, and your target's, side by side (focus on the left, target on the right). Each shows what Where to show puts on such a mob, and its marks.
- Everything in the preview moves by mouse. Drag a mark, the aggro eye or the range number or light to move it; drag it onto another side of the health bar (for the range, of the icons) and that side lights up, so drop it there to put it there. A mark holds still while you drag it. The mouse wheel sets sizes, shift-drag an icon to move the icon rows, and click a mark or the eye to open its settings.
- Nameplate marks: the target mark, the focus mark and the aggro eye each have a tab in one section. Each mark keeps all its own settings, and Copy place and bob gives the other mark the same side, offsets and bob (its shape, color, size and outline stay its own).
- Nearest the plate: when your target is also your focus and both marks are on one side, choose which mark sits nearest the nameplate. The other sits just beyond it, and the pair moves as a group.
- Each mark bobs by its own Bob setting, also when the two sit together, so a still target mark with a bobbing focus mark works. With the same bob speed and distance they bob in step; otherwise an outer mark that bobs sits a little further out, so it never touches the other.
- Where to show: the All column is now Others, every enemy nameplate but your target's and your focus's, which go by their own Target and Focus columns. Where All was ticked, Target and Focus are now ticked too, so nothing changes until you untick them.
- New settings: Eye side puts the aggro eye on any side of the health bar (or of the target panel's icons), and Range left / right and Range up / down move the range number or light.
- The settings column is wider, and the options' text is a size bigger.
- `/rangelens plates`, `/rangelens platedistance` and `/rangelens aggro` now switch every nameplate column together.
